📘 DSA Guide: Queue Data Structure & FIFO Principle

1. Problem Statement & FIFO Overview

In a computer lab with multiple students sharing a single printer, print requests must be serviced fairly in the exact order they were received. This scheduling policy is known as First-In, First-Out (FIFO), modeled by the Queue data structure.

3. Demonstration Scenario (Lab Viva-Voce Walkthrough)

1. Submit JOB-101: Sayali — DSA_Assignment.pdf — 5 pages. 2. Submit JOB-102: Rahul — OS_Notes.pdf — 10 pages. 3. Submit JOB-103: Priya — DBMS_Project.pdf — 7 pages. 4. Queue State: [FRONT: JOB-101] ───► [JOB-102] ───► [REAR: JOB-103] 5. Click 'Print Next': JOB-101 is dequeued and printed FIRST. 6. Submit JOB-104 while printing: JOB-104 enters at the REAR, behind JOB-103. 7. Result: Demonstrates strict FIFO ordering — older requests are never overtaken!
